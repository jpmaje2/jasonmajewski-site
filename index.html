Natural Light Treadmower
Close ×
For do-it-yourselfers, a cold Natural Light in the garage has long been a reward for a lawn well mowed. But when grass isn’t growing and winter weather keeps everyone stuck indoors, how can dedicated DIYers enjoy that same summer ritual while keeping their physiques in peak mowing condition?

The Natty Light TreadMower.

Part turfed treadmill. Part lawnmower. The TreadMower was designed to get bods ready for the sod, perfectly simulating the tough-but-satisfying challenge of maintaining a lawn to the highest Natty standards.

We really made a legit functioning turfed treadmill. And we filmed real-life first person mowing to create a Peloton-like mowing experience. We even released a line of lawn fitness apparel.

People loved it. Press loved it. And it changed the game for lawn fitness training.

Cornett Brand ID
Close ×
For years, the Cornett brand was purposely simple—meant to keep the focus on the work. But we needed to tell our story better. As a small independent agency in Lexington, KY, people often underestimate us or are surprised by what we bring to the table. And as a proudly female-owned agency, we’re actively defying our industry’s traditional structures.

So we set out to build a bold, distinct brand identity—a redefinition of our values, ethos, positioning, look, feel and tone.

We found our inspiration in the wild, weird world of carnivorous plants. Rather than waiting patiently for growth, they create their own circumstances. They defy the traditional path of evolution and thrive entirely on their own terms. I came across the phrase “Break the Natural Order” in an article about Darwin’s studies of carnivorous plants—and immediately loved it as a mantra, especially for an agency known for helping established brands find new advantages in crowded categories.

Our brand mark, Oddrey, is a one-eyed Venus flytrap—watchful, strategic and deadly effective. We worked with illustrator Ana Miminoshvili on Oddrey and a full set of custom illustrations, layered with botanical motifs and vivid gradients that reflect both the serious and playful sides of our personality.

I’m most proud of drawing all of this from an unexpected place, yet it all felt so distinctly “us.”

NerdWallet Pizza on a Turntable
Close ×
NerdWallet helps people make smarter financial decisions.

So, we helped them build a campaign that put their money where their mouth is...

Instead of spending big bucks on production for a national holiday campaign, we dug into the conceptual bargain bin so we could use that budget to give 25 people $100k in life-changing debt forgiveness.

An ugly frozen pizza on a turntable was about the best we could do. Or a hamster in a high chair. And a green screen. And a guy just telling you what you need to know.

It’s not going to win any Lions. But for a quick-turn campaign it got attention and drove results—900,000+ entries. And everyone who didn’t win still walked away with the financial tools to help knock out some debt.

Genius.

“I Shouldn’t Be Here...”

This campaign laid out the stakes that many patients face when treated at UK HealthCare. Life or death circumstances. Left searching for answers. Finding them at UK, and coming out on the other side with an entirely new lease on life. We wanted people to feel the pride and perseverance of UK’s patients and caregivers, and leave inspired that there’s hope even when a diagnosis feels bleak.
“When It Matters Most”

We can talk about medical advancements, research, breakthroughs. But at the end of the day, what that all really means is more time with the people we love. In this campaign, we looked to contextualize the experience patients have with UK HealthCare as only one moment in the grander scheme of a lifetime.

We used a family’s real life home videos and photo albums to tell the story. And recorded our own take on “You’re All I Need to Get By” by Marvin Gaye and Tammi Terrell for the soundtrack.
“Extraordinary Care”

This one was simple—drop people into the very real and raw moments experienced at UK HealthCare every day.
Holler Cannabis Co. Brand ID
Close ×
We were approached by Limestone Processing, Kentucky’s first and only Kentucky-based cannabis cultivators, to build a brand ID that would distinguish them from the many outside brands that would be entering the state.

Holler Cannabis Co. was inspired by the lived experience of hard-working Kentuckians, paying close attention to the little ways in which they find joy, respite and make long days a little shorter. A brand that feels at home in back pockets and gloveboxes.

We wanted to give Holler a lived-in feel with plenty of Kentucky-inspired Americana, from rolling green hills and valleys, morning fog burning off the rising sun, a cap sitting on the dash of a vintage American pony car.

We built the core brand alongside Holler Primo, bringing a more polished interpretation of the ID for an elevated product line.

Extending the Kentucky-inspired ID even further, we built a sub-brand, Moonbow Gummies inspired by one of the state’s natural wonders: the Cumberland Falls Moonbow.

Moonbow has become the #1 selling cannabis gummy brand in the state.

And Governor Andy Beshear even said “I love the name” when attending the ribbon cutting event.

Visit Dublin
Close ×
No, not that Dublin. The one in...Ohio.

...And the city I grew up in.

We got approached to take some big swings for Visit Dublin, OH to earn attention for what is actually an amazing destination for an easy family getaway.

Over the years, we’ve executed earned stunts that got outsized conversation for a small destination. For “The Other Dublin,” we offered free pints to any Irish Dubliner who would make the trip across the pond to join us for St. Paddy’s Day. We also orchestrated the World’s Largest Human Shamrock.

In the campaign space, we reimagined the city as a wholesome 90s sitcom.

Since, Dublin has continued thriving not just as a place to grow up, but as a legit regional tourism destination.
